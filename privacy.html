Privacy

Last updated: 15 November 2026.

Who runs this site

Kol Tregaskes runs Ghost in the Models. For privacy questions or rights requests, use the existing contact route or Kol's public profile. A site email address has not yet been verified, so none is published here.

Hosting and request data

The site is configured for GitHub Pages hosting. GitHub may process technical request data, including IP addresses, when serving pages and protecting its service. GitHub controls its platform logs. Kol has not verified their retention period or his access to them. See GitHub's privacy statement for its handling of service data. The verified Pages publishing source is legacy, main:/. The live site publishes from the main branch root and is not governed by the Actions artifact allowlist. Switching that source needs Kol's separate approval.

Serving the site, keeping it secure and answering messages are the purposes for any associated personal data. Where Kol processes that data, the intended basis is his legitimate interest in operating the publication and responding to readers, subject to your rights. This notice does not claim that Kol maintains a visitor database or receives GitHub's raw logs.

Fonts, scripts, images and links

Pages request stylesheets from fonts.googleapis.com and font files from fonts.gstatic.com, both Google services. These requests disclose technical connection information, including your IP address, to Google. Google may process that data outside the UK. Its retention and transfer arrangements are governed by its policies; see Google Fonts privacy information.

Those are the third-party resource hosts used by the current built pages. Site scripts, images and video are served with the site. No third-party script or embedded frame is included in the current publication. Inline SVG artwork uses namespace identifiers which do not themselves make network requests.

Essays and navigation link to other websites. Following those links takes you to a different service with its own privacy terms. An outbound link is not an embedded resource. Your browser may also retain history and cache files according to your own settings.

Storage and analytics

The current site code does not set cookies or use localStorage or sessionStorage. Reading controls change the current page in memory. Contact configuration is fetched from the same site. No analytics code or collector is active today. Cookieless analytics is planned but is not active; this notice must be updated before it is enabled.

There is no on-site account, newsletter signup, payment form or message form. If you contact Kol through another service, that service processes your message under its own terms. No fixed message-retention period is asserted here; any handling and retention by Kol must be confirmed when you make contact. Do not send sensitive personal information through a public channel.

Your rights and complaints

Under UK GDPR, depending on the circumstances, you can request access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing or data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. If processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it. These rights have conditions and exemptions. Use the contact route above to ask about data Kol holds; GitHub and Google handle requests about data they control through their own privacy routes.

You can complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office. See the ICO's guidance for the public. The ICO route remains available if contacting Kol does not resolve your concern.
